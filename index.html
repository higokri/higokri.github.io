Biography

I am a Master's student in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Pusan National University, working at CVSP Lab. My research interests lie in Vision-Language Models and efficient Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). I focus on making MLLMs more hardware-efficient through visual token pruning, knowledge distillation, and KV cache compression.

My current research focuses include:
  • Visual Token Pruning: Reducing visual tokens for efficient MLLM inference.
  • Knowledge Distillation: Compressing large models into smaller, efficient ones.
  • KV Cache Compression: Efficient key-value cache management for LLMs.
  • Robot Vision: Efficient architectures for robot vision systems.
