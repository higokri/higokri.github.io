Biography

I'm a master's student working on hardware-efficient Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) in the Department of Electronics Engineering at Pusan National University, working at the CVSP Lab under the supervision of Prof. Kyeongbo Kong. My goal is to make these models lightweight, fast, and reliable enough to run on edge devices (e.g. mobile, robotics). I'm particularly interested in uncovering how MLLMs work internally, and in using that understanding to derive general principles rather than task-specific heuristics.
